=== QUICK REFERENCE: What to look for in EXPLAIN ANALYZE ===
  - "Seq Scan" with many "rows removed by filter" -> needs an index
  - "Nested Loop" with high "loops" count -> consider hash/merge join
  - "Sort Method: external merge" -> work_mem too small, spilling to disk
  - "Buffers: shared read" >> "shared hit" -> cache is cold or too small
  - "actual time" much higher than "cost" estimate -> stale statistics (run ANALYZE)
  - "Hash Batches: N" where N > 1 -> hash spilled to disk (increase work_mem)
