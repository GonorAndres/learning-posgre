=== 7. CLOUD SQL: What you CAN and CANNOT tune ===

CAN change (via Database Flags):
  - max_connections, shared_buffers, work_mem
  - log_min_duration_statement, log_statement
  - autovacuum_* settings (per-table too)
  - random_page_cost, effective_cache_size
  - synchronous_commit (set to "off" for analytics workloads)

CANNOT change:
  - wal_level (always "replica" for HA)
  - max_wal_size (managed by Cloud SQL)
  - archive_mode/archive_command (managed for PITR)
  - full_page_writes (always on)
  - ssl configuration (always enforced)
  - pg_hba.conf (managed via IAM and Authorized Networks)

IMPORTANT: Cloud SQL handles checkpoints, WAL archival, and
replication automatically. Focus tuning on query-level settings
(work_mem, effective_cache_size) and autovacuum thresholds.
